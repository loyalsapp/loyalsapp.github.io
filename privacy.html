Loyals Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

Loyals shows you changes in your Instagram followers: who unfollowed you, who's new, and who doesn't follow you back. It is built so that your information stays on your iPhone.

Signing in to Instagram

  • You sign in on Instagram's own website. When you connect Instagram, Loyals opens instagram.com's sign-in page inside the app. Loyals does not read what you type there and never sees or stores your password.
  • Your sign-in stays on your iPhone. After you sign in, Instagram's sign-in cookies are kept by iOS's built-in web browser component on your device, the same way Safari keeps them. Loyals uses them only to load your own lists from Instagram. They are never sent to us.
  • Signing out in Loyals › Settings removes Instagram's cookies and website data from the app.

What Loyals reads

  • Your own Instagram username, Instagram account ID and follower and following counts.
  • Your own followers list and following list, with each account's username, display name, profile picture link and Instagram account ID, as Instagram shows them to you.
  • When someone seems to have unfollowed you, whether that account still follows you, so a list Instagram sent incompletely isn't mistaken for unfollows.
  • Who liked and commented on your own most recent posts (about six), so it can show which accounts engage with you most and which followers never do. It reads who engaged, not the comments' text, and at most once a day.

Loyals reads only. It never posts, comments, follows, unfollows, likes or messages anyone, and it does not read your messages, stories or anyone else's lists.

Where your data goes

  • It stays on your device. Your lists are stored on your iPhone so each check can be compared with the one before it. Like other app data, they are included in your iPhone backups if you have backups turned on.
  • Nothing is sent to us. Loyals has no servers, no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never receive your Instagram data, your sign-in, your contacts or your location.
  • Requests go straight to Instagram. To read your lists, your iPhone contacts Instagram directly, the same way it does when you browse instagram.com, and profile pictures load from Instagram's image servers. Instagram sees these requests as coming from your account, and Meta's own privacy policy applies to them.
  • Data file import (optional). If you import the file Instagram provides through "Download your information" instead of signing in, Loyals reads the followers and following lists inside it on your device in the same way.
  • Notifications are local. Reminders you turn on are scheduled on your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. Loyals receives only Apple's confirmation that your subscription is active. We never see your payment details. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple ID settings.

Deleting your data

Loyals › Settings › Delete all data removes every saved list and check from your iPhone and signs out of Instagram. Deleting the app does the same. Because we don't hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Loyals is not directed to children under 13.
