Loyals

Someone unfollowed you. Loyals tells you who.

Sign in to Instagram once, on Instagram's own page, and Loyals checks your followers for you every day. No files to download, no spreadsheets, no guessing.

What you get

  • Who unfollowed you, every name, every time.
  • Top admirers: the people who like and comment on your posts the most.
  • Secret admirers: accounts engaging with your posts without following you.
  • Ghost followers: followers who never like or comment on anything.
  • Don't follow you back, fans, mutuals, new followers, and a full history of every check.

Private by design

No servers, no accounts, no analytics. Your sign-in and your lists stay on your iPhone, and Loyals only ever reads: it never posts, follows, unfollows, likes, comments or messages.
