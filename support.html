Loyals Support

Loyals shows you who unfollowed you on Instagram, who your real admirers are, and which followers never engage. Everything runs on your iPhone.

Email support

We answer within a day. Please include your iPhone model and what you saw on screen.

Common questions

  • Loyals says Instagram asked it to wait. Instagram limits how often any app, including its own website, can read your lists. Loyals backs off automatically and tries again later. You don't need to do anything.
  • Instagram asked me to confirm it's me. That's Instagram's normal security check. Confirm it on Instagram's app or site; Loyals pauses checks on that account for two days afterwards so it doesn't happen again.
  • Someone shows as an unfollower but still follows me. Loyals double-checks every unfollower one by one before listing them, and a username change is shown as a rename, not an unfollow. If you still see a mistake, email us the two usernames and we'll look into it.
  • How do I sign out or delete my data? Settings › Sign out of Instagram, or Settings › Delete all data. Deleting the app removes everything too; nothing is stored anywhere else.
  • Does Loyals have my password? No. You sign in on Instagram's own page inside the app, and Instagram's sign-in cookies stay on your iPhone.
  • How do I cancel Loyals Pro? iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Loyals. Apple handles billing and refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com.
